# Privacy Policy for UltraStream

**Last Updated:** September 8, 2026

Welcome to UltraStream. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and how it is safeguarded when you use our Android application ("UltraStream").

## 1. Core Philosophy: No Personal Data Collection
UltraStream is designed as a standalone, client-side media utility player and metadata aggregator. **We do not collect, store, or share any of your personal information (such as your name, email address, phone number, or location) on our servers.** 

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
To provide a seamless user experience, UltraStream saves certain data strictly locally on your device's internal storage. This includes:
*   **Watch History & Playlists:** Your viewing progress, saved collections, and custom playlists.
*   **App Settings:** UI preferences, player settings, and theme configurations.
*   **Add-on Configurations:** URLs and settings of community add-ons you choose to install.

This data never leaves your device unless you manually choose to export or back it up. We have no access to your local database.

## 3. Third-Party Integrations and API Keys
UltraStream allows you to link third-party services to enhance your experience (e.g., Real-Debrid, AllDebrid, Premiumize, Trakt.tv, Simkl). 
*   **API Keys & Tokens:** When you authorize these services, the authentication tokens or API keys are stored securely and locally on your device. 
*   **Direct Communication:** The app communicates directly with these third-party providers. Your data is subject to the respective privacy policies of these third-party services. We do not intercept or route this traffic through our servers.

## 4. Third-Party Add-ons & Scrapers
UltraStream is a "blank shell" media player. It does not host, provide, or distribute any audiovisual content. 
*   Users may manually install third-party community add-ons or scrapers.
*   When using these add-ons, your IP address and search queries may be visible to the creators of those add-ons or the servers hosting the media files. 
*   We do not control and are not responsible for the data collection practices of third-party add-ons.

## 5. Device Permissions Explained
To function correctly, UltraStream requires certain standard Android permissions:
*   **Network/Internet Access:** Required to fetch metadata, stream media from your configured sources, and download updates.
*   **Storage (Read/Write):** Required *only* if you choose to use the offline download manager to save media files to your device.
*   **Foreground Service/WakeLock:** Required to prevent the system from killing the app while a video is playing or a download is in progress in the background.
*   **Notifications:** Used to show playback controls and download progress.

## 6. Analytics and Crash Reporting
Currently, UltraStream does not use invasive third-party analytics trackers (like Google Analytics) or advertising SDKs. The app is 100% ad-free. If the app crashes, Android system crash logs may be generated, which you can voluntarily choose to share via your device's built-in reporting system.

## 7. Legal Disclaimer Regarding Content
UltraStream acts solely as a utility player. We do not track what media you consume. The responsibility of ensuring that the content accessed via user-installed add-ons is legally acquired lies entirely with the user.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app's features or legal requirements. We will notify users of any significant changes via in-app release notes or by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this document.
